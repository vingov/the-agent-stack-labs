The official upstream permission tests were inspected as source during research, not executed. The binary was taken from the official release and its digest checked. Research compared 22 relevant files with v1.18.34: 21 identical; the only difference was additional provider headers in `session/llm/request.ts`, without changes to its permission filtering. A separate comparison also found `packages/core/src/shell.ts` identical. This bounded comparison supports retaining the series baseline; it does not prove whole-release equivalence.

Reading or checking these receipts is not an independent rerun or authentication of the original execution. No model inference occurred.
